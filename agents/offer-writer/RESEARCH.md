# Offers & Outreach agent — research notes

Evidence base for `.claude/agents/offer-writer.md`. Collected October 2026. Most outreach numbers come from vendors who sell email tools; treat them as **directional**, not precise.

## 1. The business model to copy (France)

- **Mandataire model**: the intermediary sells a private owner's car without ever owning it, and is paid only on sale.
- **Carizy** (owned by Renault 2018, Cofidis Group since Feb 2024): inspection at the seller's home, **5-week exclusivity**, commission reported at **~5–6% of sale price**, due only if the car sells (single aggregator source — verify). [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/carizy), [Renault Group](https://media.renaultgroup.com/?p=73684)
- **CapCar** (formerly Kyump): written mandate setting estimated price, commission, included services and an **8-week** term; seller keeps the car until final signature; listing pushed to **20+ ad sites**; claims sellers get **~20% more than a dealer** (company claim, unverified). Agents earn about **€800 per car sold**. [CapCar guide](https://www.capcar.fr/blog/vendre-sa-voiture-via-une-agence-automobile-guide-complet), [CapCar jobs](https://www.capcar.fr/offre-emploi/mandataire-automobile-hf)

**Takeaway for offers**: "no sale, no fee", "you keep your car until it sells", a fixed mandate period, and the "more than the dealer" comparison are already proven selling points.

## 2. Hong Kong market facts that shape the offer

- **Transfer of ownership**: the new owner must submit form **TD25** to the Transport Department within **72 hours**, or the transfer is incomplete and the old owner stays liable. Sellers are advised not to hand over the original registration document until the transfer is registered. → Strong pain point a broker removes. [TD25 form](https://www.td.gov.hk/filemanager/en/content_5118/td25_e-fillable_eng.pdf), [Kwiksure](https://kwiksure.com/blog/car-ownership-transfer/)
- **Where cars are sold**: **28car** is described as the most popular used-car site (Traditional Chinese, dated interface); **Carousell** runs Carousell Autos and says ~1 in 5 Hongkongers use it. [MoneySmart](https://blog.moneysmart.hk/en/living/hong-kong-used-cars/), [Sing Tao](https://www.stheadline.com/ad-enterprise/3491904/)
- **Trust problem**: the HK Automobile Association has warned that small used-car dealers can misrepresent accident history (notably on Japanese imports); 28car's own guide lists mileage tampering among common scams. → Inspection + documented history is a strong buyer-side offer. [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/article/968500/when-old-rubber-hits-road), [Kwiksure 28car guide](https://kwiksure.com/blog/28car-user-guide)
- **EV tax change**: FRT concessions for electric private cars (incl. One-for-One Replacement) **ended 31 March 2026**; >70% of new private-car registrations were electric in 2025. → Used EVs registered before the change may now look relatively cheaper than new ones; a timely hook for sellers and buyers. [HK Gov press release](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202602/25/P2026022500288.htm), [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/hong-kong-news/article/325237/Budget-2026-Tax-breaks-end-for-electric-private-cars-as-EV-adoption-grows)
- **Channel**: a Meta-commissioned Kantar survey (500 HK adults) found **71%** message businesses on WhatsApp weekly; about half prefer WhatsApp to contact businesses vs **29% email** and **28% phone**. Biased source, but consistent with practice. [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/innovation/article/66736/Customers-prefer-WhatsApp-over-calls), [KPay Digital 2025](https://go.kpay-group.com/blog/2025-digital-hk-report)

Gaps — no reliable data found on: HK used-car market size, 28car/Carousell seller fees, typical HK dealer trade-in discount. Collect these from the business's own deals as soon as possible; they become the strongest proof.

## 3. What makes outreach get replies

- **Length**: Boomerang (40M+ emails) found 50–125 words is the sweet spot; reply rate falls ~1% per extra 25 words. Instantly reports top performers (>10% reply) keep emails **under 80 words**. [Boomerang](https://blog.boomerangapp.com/2016/02/7-tips-for-getting-more-responses-to-your-emails-with-data/), [Instantly](https://instantly.ai/blog/how-to-improve-your-cold-email-reply-rate)
- **Reading level**: ~3rd-grade writing had the best reply rate (~53%), 36% higher than college-level writing. [Boomerang via VOA](https://learningenglish.voanews.com/amp/tips-for-email/3842882.html)
- **Questions**: emails with 1–3 questions were ~50% more likely to get a reply than none; 8+ questions hurt. [Boomerang](https://blog.boomerangapp.com/2016/02/7-tips-for-getting-more-responses-to-your-emails-with-data/)
- **Benchmarks**: average cold-email reply rate ~3.4% (Instantly 2026); 4–6% for targeted campaigns, falling to ~2% at 1,000+ recipients (Belkins). [Saleshandy](https://saleshandy.com/blog/cold-email-statistics/), [Digital Bloom](https://thedigitalbloom.com/learn/cold-outbound-reply-rate-benchmarks/)
- **Personalisation and signals**: real personalisation beats generic blasts; reaching people at a trigger moment (signal-based) matters even more. [Smartlead](https://www.smartlead.ai/benchmarks/how-much-to-personalize-cold-email)
- **Follow-ups**: a large share of replies (roughly 40–60% depending on source) comes after the first email → always plan a sequence. [Saleshandy](https://saleshandy.com/blog/cold-email-statistics/)

## 4. What makes an offer hard to refuse

- **Value Equation** (Alex Hormozi, *$100M Offers*): value = (dream outcome × perceived likelihood) ÷ (time delay × effort & sacrifice). A framework, not a study — but it maps cleanly onto the broker's service.
- **Guarantees / risk reversal**: case studies report conversion lifts of ~21–29% from visible guarantees; one found net revenue lift of ~6.5% after refunds. A test found a 30-day guarantee beat a 37-day one (odd numbers may reduce trust). Vendor case studies, not controlled research. [Conversion Rate Experts](https://conversion-rate-experts.com/button-microcopy-win-report/), [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/money-back-guarantee), [QuickSprout](https://www.quicksprout.com/what-converts-better-free-trial-versus-money-back-guarantee/)
- **Known persuasion principles** used in the agent: giving a reason ("because…"), anchoring against the real alternative, loss aversion (what they lose by waiting or selling to a dealer), social proof, and a tiny first commitment.

## 5. Compliance (Hong Kong) — check with a lawyer before scaling

- **Trade Descriptions Ordinance (Cap. 362)** — misleading claims, false scarcity and misleading omissions can be offences.
- **Unsolicited Electronic Messages Ordinance (Cap. 593)** — commercial email/SMS/WhatsApp must identify the sender and offer an unsubscribe option.
- **Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486)** — using personal data (e.g. contact details copied from 28car listings) for direct marketing requires notice and consent.
