## 3. Why GEO matters (market proof points)

- **Gartner (Feb 2024) forecast**: traditional search volume to drop 25% by 2026 — a *forecast*, only partly borne out. [LLM Pulse](https://llmpulse.ai/blog/state-of-geo/)
- **Zero-click**: ~58–60% of Google searches end without a click; Pew data cited showing CTR falls from 15% to 8% when an AI Overview appears. [Peec AI stats](https://peec.ai/ai-search-geo-statistics)
- **Scale**: ChatGPT ~900M weekly active users (Feb 2026); ChatGPT referral traffic to sites +206% YoY (Semrush/Datos). AI-referred retail visitors converted 42% better (Adobe, Mar 2026, secondhand). [ContextBolt](https://contextbolt.com/ai-search-statistics/)
- **GEO works**: Princeton et al., *GEO: Generative Engine Optimization* (KDD 2024) — adding citations, quotations and statistics lifted visibility in generative engines by up to ~40%. [arXiv 2311.09735](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735)
- **Region**: GEO is rising across ASEAN and HK. [ASEAN Briefing](https://www.aseanbriefing.com/news/geo-marketing-is-rising-across-asean/), [UD HK](https://ud.hk/en/blogs/insight/article/2026-05-01-geo-generative-engine-optimization-hk)

## 4. What gets replies

- **Length**: 50–125 words is the sweet spot; reply rate falls ~1% per extra 25 words (Boomerang, 40M+ emails). Top performers (>10% reply) stay under 80 words (Instantly). [Boomerang](https://blog.boomerangapp.com/2016/02/7-tips-for-getting-more-responses-to-your-emails-with-data/), [Instantly](https://instantly.ai/blog/how-to-improve-your-cold-email-reply-rate)
- **Reading level**: ~3rd-grade writing replied best (~53%), +36% vs college-level. [VOA summary](https://learningenglish.voanews.com/amp/tips-for-email/3842882.html)
- **Questions**: 1–3 questions → ~50% more replies than none; 8+ hurts.
- **Benchmarks**: avg cold reply ~3.4% (Instantly 2026); targeted 4–6%, ~2% at 1,000+ recipients (Belkins). [Saleshandy](https://saleshandy.com/blog/cold-email-statistics/), [Digital Bloom](https://thedigitalbloom.com/learn/cold-outbound-reply-rate-benchmarks/)
- **Signals and personalisation**: trigger-based, genuinely personal outreach beats generic blasts by a wide margin. [Smartlead](https://www.smartlead.ai/benchmarks/how-much-to-personalize-cold-email)
- **Follow-ups**: roughly 40–60% of replies come after the first email.
- **HK channel habits**: ~half of HK consumers prefer WhatsApp to contact businesses vs 29% email (Meta-commissioned Kantar survey) — relevant for warm contacts; email stays the B2B default. [The Standard](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/innovation/article/66736/Customers-prefer-WhatsApp-over-calls)

## 5. What makes an offer hard to refuse

- **Value Equation** (Hormozi, *$100M Offers*): value = (dream outcome × perceived likelihood) ÷ (time delay × effort). A framework, not a study.
- **Show, don't tell**: a prospect's own scan finding is the strongest proof Geoneo has; it is specific, verifiable and about them.
- **Risk reversal**: visible guarantees lifted conversion ~21–29% in case studies (net ~6.5% after refunds in one); round periods (30 days) beat odd ones. Vendor case studies. [Conversion Rate Experts](https://conversion-rate-experts.com/button-microcopy-win-report/), [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/money-back-guarantee)
- Principles used: giving a reason ("because…"), anchoring against the real alternative, loss aversion (competitor recommended instead), social proof, tiny first commitment (free scan).

## 6. Compliance — check with a lawyer before scaling

- **Hong Kong**: Unsolicited Electronic Messages Ordinance (Cap. 593) — sender ID + unsubscribe; PDPO (Cap. 486) — direct-marketing consent for personal data; Trade Descriptions Ordinance (Cap. 362) — no misleading claims.
- **Singapore**: Spam Control Act — unsubscribe facility, accurate headers for bulk commercial email; PDPA — business contact information largely exempt, Do Not Call registry applies to calls/SMS/messages to SG numbers.
